Experience Founding Engineer

AutoPallet Robotics

A YC-backed warehouse automation startup building robots that pick, sort and palletize cases. I joined as an intern, stayed on full time as a founding engineer, and return next summer.

Summer 2026 – present San Francisco, CA Warehouse automation
Y Combinator backed Ongoing

Highlights

  • Mega Gripper. Designed and built AutoPallet's current vacuum gripper end to end: one gripper that picks both small and large parcels. I have hand-built 15 grippers in all.
  • Endurance testing. Designed the test chamber and its rigs, which have logged more than 370,000 cycles between them, including a single 210,000-cycle run.
  • Production. Run robot production: the build instructions, an inventory system tracking 265 parts, and a wall of 15 3D printers.
  • Electronics and analysis. Designed custom PCBs, wrote firmware for our grippers and test rigs, and ran the FEA and pull tests behind a redesign that raised holding force by 58%.
