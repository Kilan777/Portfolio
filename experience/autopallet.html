AutoPallet Robotics

A YC-backed warehouse automation startup building robots that pick, sort and palletize cases. I own mechanical design and mechatronic prototyping.

2026 – present Warehouse automation
Y Combinator backed Ongoing

AutoPallet Robotics builds robots for the inside of a warehouse: picking cases off pallets, sorting them and building new pallets. I joined as a founding engineer and work on the mechanical design and the mechatronic prototypes that turn the concept into hardware that runs all day.

The role

At a company this size the mechanical engineer does everything from the first CAD sketch to the part that ships: designing the mechanisms, ordering and building them, wiring the electronics, writing the firmware that exercises them, and running them until they break so the next revision does not.

Main project

The case-handling end effector and the mechanisms around it. This is where I spend most of my time: the design, the test rigs that cycle it thousands of times, and the revisions that come out of those tests.

Other work

  • Endurance test rigs: automated cycle-test stations that run mechanisms continuously and log every cycle, so wear shows up on the bench before it shows up in a warehouse.
  • Custom electronics: small sensor and interface boards where an off-the-shelf part did not fit.
  • Rapid prototyping: a bank of 3D printers and the tooling to keep them producing parts for the fleet.

What I learned

How to design for a machine that has to run unattended: what wears, what shakes loose, and how to find out early. And how a small team ships hardware fast without skipping the testing.
